# Build a Complete AI Answer Grader & Exam Evaluation Dashboard

## 1. Project Objective

Build a web-based **AI Answer Grader Dashboard** that evaluates student exam answers against teacher-provided reference answers and official marking schemes.

The system should not simply reward longer answers. It should evaluate whether the student answer contains the **important concepts, keywords, points, reasoning, and required elements** present in the teacher's reference answer and marking scheme.

The main purpose of the project is to investigate:

> **Can an AI/LLM grade exam answers fairly compared with a human teacher?**

The application should therefore provide both:

1. **AI-based grading**
2. **Teacher/reference-based evaluation**

and compare them using clear analytics.

# 5. AI Evaluation Engine

The AI grader should evaluate the student answer using multiple dimensions rather than only word count.

Calculate:

### A. Keyword Coverage

Percentage of important keywords/concepts present in the student answer.

Example:

Required concepts = 10

Student contains = 7

Keyword coverage:

70%

---

### B. Concept Coverage

Determine whether the student has communicated the important concepts even if the exact keyword is absent.

Example:

Reference:

"Controls the supply of money in the economy."

Student:

"Regulates the amount of money available in the economy."

These should receive strong semantic similarity even though the wording differs.

---

### C. Marking Scheme Coverage

Check each individual point required by the official marking scheme.

Example:

| Required Point | Student Included? | Score |
| -------------- | ----------------- | ----: |
| Point 1        | Yes               |     1 |
| Point 2        | Yes               |     1 |
| Point 3        | No                |     0 |

---

### D. Relevance

Determine whether the answer actually addresses the question.

A long answer containing irrelevant information should NOT receive a high score.

---

### E. Accuracy

Determine whether the information provided is factually correct.

Incorrect statements should reduce the score.

---

### F. Explanation Quality

For questions requiring explanation, evaluate:

* clarity
* logical reasoning
* completeness
* use of examples where required

---

### G. Conciseness / Relevance

Do NOT automatically reward longer answers.

A short answer containing all required points should be able to receive full marks.

A long answer containing irrelevant or incorrect information should receive a lower score.

---

# 6. Final AI Score

Generate a final score according to the official marking scheme.

For example:

```text
Maximum Marks: 5

Keyword/Concept Coverage: 82%
Marking Scheme Coverage: 4/5
Relevance: 90%
Accuracy: 80%
Explanation Quality: 85%

AI Score: 4/5
```

The official marks must remain the primary basis for the final score.

Do not allow word count or answer length to dominate the score.

# 8. AI vs Teacher Comparison

For every answer calculate:

### Score Gap

```text
AI Score − Teacher Score
```

Example:

AI = 4

Teacher = 3

Gap = +1

This means AI **overestimated** the answer.

If:

AI = 2

Teacher = 4

Gap = -2

This means AI **underestimated** the answer.

Display:

* AI Score
* Teacher Score
* Score Gap
* Absolute Gap

---

# 11. Bias Detection

Create an automated fairness analysis.

The system should detect patterns such as:

### Length Bias

AI gives higher scores to longer answers even when they contain irrelevant information.

### Keyword Bias

AI gives high scores because many keywords appear, even when the concepts are poorly explained or incorrectly used.

### Short-Answer Penalty

AI gives low scores to concise answers despite covering all marking-scheme points.

### Semantic Matching Failure

AI fails to recognize correct answers because the student uses different wording.

### Irrelevance Reward

AI rewards long answers containing information unrelated to the question.

### Factual Error Reward

AI gives high scores despite incorrect claims.

# 19. Overall Accuracy Calculation

Calculate:

### Mean Absolute Error (MAE)

```text
MAE = Average(|AI Score − Teacher Score|)
```

### Agreement Rate

Percentage of answers where:

```text
AI Score = Teacher Score
```

### Overestimation Rate

Percentage where:

```text
AI Score > Teacher Score
```

### Underestimation Rate

Percentage where:

```text
AI Score < Teacher Score
```

### Correlation

Calculate correlation between:

* AI Score
* Teacher Score

Also calculate correlation between:

* Word Count
* AI Score

and:

* Word Count
* Teacher Score

This is important for identifying length bias.

# 26. Important Grading Rules

The AI must follow these rules strictly:

### Rule 1

**Do not reward length by itself.**

### Rule 2

**Do not require exact keyword matching.**

Recognize semantically equivalent expressions.

### Rule 3

**Use the official marking scheme as the primary scoring framework.**

### Rule 4

A concise answer can receive full marks.

### Rule 5

A long answer can receive low marks.

### Rule 6

Irrelevant information should not increase the score.

### Rule 7

Incorrect information should reduce the score where relevant.

### Rule 8

The AI should explain why marks were awarded or deducted.

### Rule 9

The AI must distinguish between:

* Missing information
* Incorrect information
* Different but correct wording
* Irrelevant information

### Rule 10

Never calculate accuracy using keyword percentage alone.

Keyword coverage should be **one component** of evaluation, not the complete grading mechanism.

---

# 27. Core Scoring Model

Use a transparent scoring framework.

Suggested evaluation:

```text
Marking Scheme Coverage       40%
Concept/Semantic Coverage     25%
Factual Accuracy              15%
Relevance                     10%
Explanation/Reasoning         10%
```

Do NOT directly add these percentages to create marks if doing so conflicts with the official marking scheme.

Instead, use these dimensions to support the AI's decision while the **official marks allocation remains the final scoring authority**.
